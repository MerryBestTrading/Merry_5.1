Who we are

A research group that became a trading technology company.

Founded in 2000, Merry began as a small trading research group and has grown into a mid-sized firm with a team of 30 professionals. Our expertise spans trading technology, quantitative research, and the development of AI-powered trading systems.

For many years we developed and refined our technology privately, working with a select group of clients and building on proprietary research and methodologies. As the system matured, we decided to introduce our strategy to a wider audience in October 2026.

Today our work spans model research, market analysis, engineering, and operational risk control. Merry 5.1 is our public-facing system; Merry 5.2 supports active clients with automated execution; Merry 6.0 has been completed and is scheduled for institutional release in May 2027.

The evolution of Merry

From an indicator bot to an AI trading platform.

Progress was not immediate. Each stage built on the lessons and capabilities of the previous one.

2000

The beginning

Merry starts as a relatively simple, indicator-based trading bot, developed inside a small German research group.

2023

The AI transition

The architecture evolves from a comprehensive Python-based system into an AI-driven trading platform, eventually becoming the agent-based system we operate today.

2026

Merry 5.0, 5.1 and 5.2

Merry 5.0 introduces AI-agent automation. Merry 5.1 refines market analysis for the public release. Merry 5.2 adds automated execution through API connectivity with Hyperliquid for active clients.

May 2027

Merry 6.0 — institutional edition

Development is complete. Merry 6.0 is a multi-agent system designed for broader market analysis, with institutional sales planned as the initial distribution model.

Governance

A hierarchy built on checks, not mythology.

Architecture, security and release readiness are controlled by engineering. Technical safety can veto a deployment.
