Defense in depth

Controls that protect the system and your data.

Security is layered: encryption, access control, monitoring, resilience, and secure engineering.

Encryption in transit

TLS 1.3 with modern cipher suites protects every connection to the site and the release download.

Encryption at rest

AES-256 encryption for stored data and backups, with keys managed separately from the data they protect.

Least-privilege access

Role-based access, multi-factor authentication, and quarterly reviews of who can reach production systems.

Continuous monitoring

Centralized logging, alerting, and a documented incident-response process with named owners.

Resilience

Tested backups, disaster-recovery procedures, and defined recovery objectives for critical services.

Secure development

Threat modeling, code review, dependency scanning, and annual independent penetration tests.

Your data, your machine

Local-first by design.

Merry 5.1 runs at 127.0.0.1 on your computer. Chart analysis and market data stay on your device; there is no account and no cloud dashboard to configure.

The only outbound connection is to Binance for market data. Merry 5.1 does not place trades for you — you stay in control of every order.
